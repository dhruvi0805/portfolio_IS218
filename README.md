## Review 1 · Career research

**Research question.** Which career direction best aligns my UX and product design skills with my goal of working across AI, technology, and business operations?

### Employer evidence

Original employer postings checked November 11, 2026. This is the access date. The skill gaps below are research hypotheses.

- **Palantir · Forward Deployed Software Engineer, New Grad – Commercial.** New York. Engineers solve technical problems with customers and build software around their needs. The posting requires programming proficiency and graduation in December 2026 or spring 2027. It lists an estimated $135,000–$145,000 annual salary. The gap to investigate is building and deploying production software. The posting supports the engineering comparison. The amount of AI work on a given project remains an open question. [Employer posting](https://jobs.lever.co/palantir/2e6b0ac8-83e9-4be5-a3aa-cf319f751728)
- **DoorDash · Product Design, Entry-Level (2027 start).** San Francisco or New York. Designers work with product, research, data, and engineering to prototype, test with users, and ship changes. The posting describes AI as part of design exploration and prototyping. The gap to investigate is documenting how AI-assisted design choices hold up in user testing. [Employer posting](https://careersatdoordash.com/jobs/product-design-entry-level-2027-start/8188161/)
- **Accenture · Entry Level Advisory Program – Sourcing & Procurement Analyst.** Procurement consulting involves analyzing spending and recommending improvements to client sourcing workflows. The posting requires an anticipated bachelor's or master's graduation no later than May 2027 and gives December 11, 2026 as its expected closing date. The gap to investigate is procurement domain knowledge and analytical consulting. This is a candidate for the third role. Confirm whether AI-enabled consulting satisfies the other-industry requirement. [Employer posting](https://www.accenture.com/us-en/careers/jobdetails?id=R00367105_en)

### Career directions

AI product design uses prototyping and user testing to decide what to build. AI-enabled operations uses data analysis and process knowledge to improve business decisions. FDE provides an engineering benchmark because the Palantir posting explicitly requires programming proficiency. These comparisons reflect the listed responsibilities. The postings give limited evidence about how teams divide their time.

**Next step.** Prototype an AI-assisted supplier comparison workflow using sample data. Test whether a reader can trace a recommendation to the data behind it. Record one revision and what the test suggests about the design and operations directions.

### Portfolio plan

**Proposed reader.** An early-career UX or product design hiring manager scanning for evidence of research, prototyping, and collaboration.

- **Bauhaus with subtle glass effects.** Use a geometric grid, strong type, and restrained color. Favor high-contrast cards so job requirements and sources are easy to scan.
- **8-bit RPG.** Use a pixel-inspired map and role cards to illustrate alternative paths. Test whether readers can find a source without learning the navigation metaphor.
- **Initial choice.** Prototype the geometric layout first. Ask a partner to locate an employer requirement and its source before deciding whether to add game-inspired navigation.

Possible short stories to develop from real projects: a research finding that changed a design, a prototype revised after user feedback, and a design tradeoff made with an engineering or business partner.
