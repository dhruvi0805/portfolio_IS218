# Employer research

Original employer postings checked November 11, 2026. This is the access date. The skill gaps below are research hypotheses.

- **Palantir · Forward Deployed Software Engineer, New Grad – Commercial.** New York. Engineers solve technical problems with customers and build software around their needs. The posting requires programming proficiency and graduation in December 2026 or spring 2027. It lists an estimated $135,000–$145,000 annual salary. The gap to investigate is building and deploying production software. The posting supports the engineering comparison. The amount of AI work on a given project remains an open question. [Employer posting](https://jobs.lever.co/palantir/2e6b0ac8-83e9-4be5-a3aa-cf319f751728)
- **DoorDash · Product Design, Entry-Level (2027 start).** San Francisco or New York. Designers work with product, research, data, and engineering to prototype, test with users, and ship changes. The posting describes AI as part of design exploration and prototyping. The gap to investigate is documenting how AI-assisted design choices hold up in user testing. [Employer posting](https://careersatdoordash.com/jobs/product-design-entry-level-2027-start/8188161/)
- **Accenture · Entry Level Advisory Program – Sourcing & Procurement Analyst.** The posting lists Atlanta, Boston, Chicago, and New York City and calls for some travel. Analysts evaluate client spending, recommend sourcing improvements, and use AI in daily procurement workflows. They are expected to design, revise, and validate AI outputs. AI duties and locations rechecked November 12, 2026. The posting requires an anticipated bachelor's or master's graduation no later than May 2027 and gives December 11, 2026 as its expected closing date. The gap to investigate is procurement domain knowledge and analytical consulting. I use this as the AI-enabled operations example because the work applies AI to sourcing and procurement in a consulting practice. [Employer posting](https://www.accenture.com/us-en/careers/jobdetails?id=R00367105_en)
