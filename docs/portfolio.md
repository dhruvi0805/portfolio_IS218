# Portfolio plan

**Proposed reader.** An early-career UX or product design hiring manager scanning for evidence of research, prototyping, and collaboration.

- **Bauhaus with subtle glass effects.** Use a geometric grid, strong type, and restrained color. Favor high-contrast cards so job requirements and sources are easy to scan.
- **8-bit RPG.** Use a pixel-inspired map and role cards to illustrate alternative paths. Test whether readers can find a source without learning the navigation metaphor.
- **Initial choice.** Prototype the geometric layout first. Ask a partner to locate an employer requirement and its source before deciding whether to add game-inspired navigation.

Possible short stories to develop from real projects: a research finding that changed a design, a prototype revised after user feedback, and a design tradeoff made with an engineering or business partner.
